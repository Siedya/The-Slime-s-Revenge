# The-Slime-s-Revenge
Hack Club Haven

Main theme: Getting down and getting back up

Main Character: Slime (player)
Main premises:
The Slime’s Revenge; A slime calles Slime Shady once had a family. But once upon a time, they were taken from him by a monster, an Ogre. The slime seeks revenge and enters the Ogre’s domain to kill it. They fight but eventually (unless ur pro player) the ogre defeats the slime. 
Unlike his family, Slime Shady respawns and comes back improved with new abilities. The slime is still in the cave and wants to track down the ogre for a rematch. Slime Shady kills enemies along the way, gaining more skills with each kill. Eventually, he tracks down the ogre and fights it once more. This time, the slime wins and honors his family.
